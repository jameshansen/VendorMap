**James Kennedy Elementary Christmas Market Vendor Setup, Guidelines & Details**
					
**Booth Requirements:**	
1. One Table measuring 6 feet by 2.5 feet will be provided 
2. There will 2.0 feet spacing between each booth	
3. Please ensure decorations maintain a festive and organized appearance. 
4. Only 2 individuals should operator and maintain each booth at all times
5. Pets are not permitted			
6. Fire Safety: Ensure all temporary structures such as stalls and booths 
comply with fire safety regulations		
7. Electrical Safety: All electrical installations, including lights and decorations, 
must be installed by qualified personnel and meet safety standards. Avoid overloading electrical circuits. 
8. Please maintain noise levels to ensure a pleasant atmosphere for all visitors. 
					
**Product Quality:**	
1. Vendors must sell safe and high-quality products that meet local regulations and standards. 
2. Food vendors must comply with hygiene and food safety regulations as administered by Fraser Health. 
Including but not limited to display of food safety certifications where applicable. 
3. Where applicable please use eco-friend practices, such as minimizing plastic use and recycling. 
					
**Custom Made Products Delivery:**
If you intend to sell custom items at your table, you must make arrangements to deliver the item to the purchaser/customer or arrange a date for pick up at James Kennedy Elementary within a reasonable timeframe. 
						
**Special Circumstances and/or Weather Conditions:**
Should there be a district closure due to strong winds, snow or rain, the Christmas 
market will be cancelled. 
					
**Refund Eligibility:**			
Refunds will be grant based on special circumstances, such as illness, or emergency. 
The deadline to request and receive a full refund is Saturday, September 12th, 2026.
					
**Cancellation Deadlines:** 			
If you are unable to make the market, please contact our Vendor Coordinator
as soon as possible. No refunds will be granted after Sunday, September 12th, 2026.
